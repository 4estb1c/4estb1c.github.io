# Margin research corpus

This directory is the reusable, public-source evidence layer for Margin. It is deliberately separate from article prose and does not contain a copy of any source article.

- `evidence.json` is machine-readable: every fact has a date/status, a direct source URL, and a caveat.
- `claim-audit.md` is a concise, full-series claim ledger: it maps each chapter’s central forecast and assumption clusters to evidence IDs. It is not a line-by-line or exhaustive numeric audit.
- `rights-and-sourcing.md` records the reproduction decision and editorial constraints.

`asOf` is the evidence cutoff, **2026-09-25**. A forecast is never silently converted into an observation. “Company-reported” includes vendor benchmark and product claims; use independent evaluations when a decision depends on a comparison.
