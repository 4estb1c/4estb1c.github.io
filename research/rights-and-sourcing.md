# Rights and sourcing decision

## Original article

The canonical *Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead* site (June 2024) displays no reuse licence. A public mirror describes the work as copyrighted by Leopold Aschenbrenner and says the author retains all rights. That is not a licence grant, but it is clear evidence against assuming permission.

**Decision:** do not publish a full copy, scraped HTML, figures, or a reconstruction whose accumulated excerpts substitute for the original. Use the canonical chapter links, independent summaries, and short quotations only when needed for criticism/commentary. Preserve original footnotes only as links or short, attributed references after permission. Seek written permission before loading full original text into the public reader.

This is a product/rights assessment, not legal advice. It is intentionally conservative because the planned public site would redistribute the whole work.

## Evidence conventions

- Prefer first-party technical, investor, government, and intergovernmental sources.
- Label fact status `observed`, `announced`, `forecast`, `company-reported`, or `policy`.
- Add the measurement boundary: model setting, benchmark subset, hardware SKU, electricity scope, or legal status.
- Treat advertised peak FLOPS, benchmark scores, and corporate commitments as non-interchangeable with deployed capability, usable training compute, or energised capacity.
